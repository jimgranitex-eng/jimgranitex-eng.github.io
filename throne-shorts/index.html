Throne_shorts

Throne_shorts is a private content-management application used by its owner to publish and manage videos on their own YouTube channels (gaming content on the Uubjin channel and the KitchenThrone channel).

The app connects to the YouTube Data API to:

  • Read channel and video metadata for the owner's channels.
  • Upload new videos to the owner's channels on a publishing schedule.

The app is used solely by its owner. It does not serve the general public, collect visitor data, or share data with third parties.
