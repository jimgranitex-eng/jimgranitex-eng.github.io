Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What this app does

Throne_shorts is a private application used by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels (gaming content on the Uubjin channel and the KitchenThrone channel). It uses the YouTube Data API with the owner's own Google account authorization to read channel/video metadata and upload videos.

Data we access

Through the YouTube Data API, the app accesses the owner's YouTube channel information, video metadata, and uploads videos — strictly for the owner's own channels, under the owner's own authorization. The app requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — view the owner's YouTube account data.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to the owner's channels.

Data we collect from visitors

This website collects no personal data from visitors. There are no accounts, no analytics, no cookies, and no tracking.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. API data obtained through Google APIs is used only to operate the app's YouTube management features and is never transferred to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

OAuth tokens are stored securely by the owner for the sole purpose of maintaining the app's connection to the owner's YouTube channels. The owner may revoke the app's access at any time via their Google account permissions page, which immediately stops all data access.
