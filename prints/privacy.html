Privacy Policy — Fine-Art Print Site

Property: Fine-art prints (Op 06 — AI-generated artwork, POD via Printful/Printify)

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Platform: self-built storefront on prints.silvius.world (decided 2026-10-09 — no Shopify). Print-on-demand via Printful (primary) / Printify (backup).

Launch gate placeholders: `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}`, `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`, `{{MAILING_ADDRESS}}`.

1. What we collect and why

Information you give us

  • Order details: name, email, shipping address, phone (only if provided), prints/sizes ordered. Needed to fulfill and ship.
  • Payment details: processed by Stripe Checkout (Stripe-hosted) — cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Card data never touches our servers.
  • Newsletter signup: email ("New prints monthly") → Kit list, double opt-in EU/CA.
  • Support messages sent to the templated contact email.

Collected automatically

  • Page views, product views, add-to-cart, checkout events (to run and improve the shop).
  • Device/browser data, approximate location from IP (country/region — used for currency and EU/California consent rules).
  • IP address (security/fraud).

Cookies and tracking — see Cookie Policy. Runs Meta Pixel, Google tags, and footprint-v2.js attribution (cookieless) [A and B — via the landing page]; no marketing tag fires in the EU until banner consent.

Never: precise location, card details, sale of personal information.

2. Who we share it with (processors)

Fulfillment partners get your address only to print and ship your order.

3. Honest data note — AI artwork and photos

Every piece is AI-generated original artwork (disclosed on each product page and the About page). We do not use customer photos for any purpose and do not request them. If you email us a photo of your hung print (e.g., a damage claim), it is used only for that claim.

4. Your rights and choices

  • Unsubscribe: one click, immediate. Order/delivery emails are transactional and continue regardless.
  • Access / correction / deletion: `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`, within 30 days (tax-required order records kept as long as law requires).
  • GDPR: basis consent (marketing) and contract (orders). CCPA: no sale of personal information; rights at `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`. Full sale/share opt-out mechanics: CCPA Do Not Sell/Share.
  • Cookies: "Cookie settings" footer link — see Cookie Policy.

5. Returns (data note)

Damaged/misprinted/wrong-item claims may require a photo of the print; shared with Printful/Printify for the reprint workflow only. Custom prints can't be restocked — stated plainly in the ToS.

6. Children

Not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

Posted here with the date; material changes announced.
