Affiliate Disclosure

Granit Dog Picks is an affiliate curator — a travel agency between you and the merchant. We find products worth your attention and point you to where you can buy them. We do not sell anything ourselves.

How we earn

When you click a product link and buy from the merchant (for example Amazon), the merchant may pay us a commission — typically a small percentage of the sale. This costs you nothing extra. The price you pay is the merchant’s price.

What we never do

  • We never take your payment — checkout happens on the merchant’s site.
  • We never see your order, payment details, or address.
  • We never ship, handle returns, or provide customer support for products — the merchant does all of that.
  • We never sell or share your personal information.

Our promise

We only feature products we’d point a friend to. A commission never changes what we recommend — if a product isn’t worth it, it doesn’t get featured, commission or not.
