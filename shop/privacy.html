Privacy Policy — Dog Store

Property: Dog store (Op 05 — self-built storefront on shop.silvius.world, US/EU shipping zones only, Printful/Printify POD fulfillment)

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Launch gate placeholders: `{{BUSINESS_NAME}}`, `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`, `{{MAILING_ADDRESS}}`.

---

1. What we collect and why

Information you give us

  • Order details: name, email, shipping address, phone (only if you provide it), and what you ordered. Needed to fulfill and ship your order.
  • Payment details: processed by Stripe Checkout (Stripe-hosted). Card data goes directly to the payment processor; we never see or store full card numbers.
  • Account/contact info: email from the newsletter signup ("Get the funniest dog stuff first"); messages you send to support email.
  • Reviews: if you leave a product review (our own reviews widget), we receive the review text, the name you display, and your email (used only to verify the review is from a real buyer).

Information collected automatically

  • Page views, products viewed, items added to cart, checkout progress (used to improve the store and recover abandoned checkouts).
  • Device/browser data, approximate location (country/region from IP — also used to enforce the US/EU-only shipping zones).
  • IP address (security and fraud screening via Stripe Radar and server logs).

Cookies and tracking — see Cookie Policy. The store runs strictly-necessary operational cookies plus advertising/analytics tags (Meta Pixel, Google tags) and footprint-v2.js attribution (cookieless). No marketing tag fires in the EU until banner consent.

We do not collect precise GPS location, and we never sell personal information.

2. Who we share it with (processors)

Fulfillment partners receive your address only to produce and ship what you ordered; their own privacy policies govern their handling.

3. Your rights and choices

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails: one click, immediate.
  • Access / correction / deletion: email `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`; handled within 30 days. Note: order records needed for tax law are kept as long as legally required.
  • GDPR (EU/UK): basis is *consent* (marketing) and *contract* (order fulfillment). Withdraw consent anytime.
  • CCPA (California): we do not sell personal information; right to know/delete/non-discrimination at `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`. Full sale/share opt-out mechanics: CCPA Do Not Sell/Share.
  • Cookies: manage anytime via "Cookie settings" in the footer — see Cookie Policy.

4. Order-related emails

Transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping/tracking, delivery) are sent regardless of marketing consent — they are part of fulfilling your order. Marketing emails only go to subscribers.

5. Returns, refunds, damage claims (data note)

Damage/misprint claims may require a photo of the item and are forwarded to the fulfillment partner (Printful/Printify) for their reprint workflow. Only the claim details are shared — nothing else.

6. Children

Not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

Posted here with the date; material changes announced.
