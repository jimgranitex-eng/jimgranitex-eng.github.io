1. What we collect and why

Information you give us

  • Account/contact info: email from the newsletter signup ("Get the funniest dog stuff first"); messages you send to support email.

Information collected automatically

  • Page views and outbound affiliate clicks (which product you tapped, when) — used to understand what curations work. No purchase data: the sale happens on the merchant’s site, which we never see.
  • Device/browser data, approximate location (country/region from IP) for analytics.
  • IP address (security and server logs).

Cookies and tracking — see Cookie Policy. The store runs strictly-necessary operational cookies plus advertising/analytics tags (Meta Pixel, Google tags) and footprint-v2.js attribution (cookieless). No marketing tag fires in the EU until banner consent.

We do not collect precise GPS location, and we never sell personal information.

2. Who we share it with

Almost nobody — and never for fulfillment, because there is none. We do not sell products, so we never share your address, payment details, or order history with anyone: there is no order history to share.

  • Newsletter provider (self-built): your email address is stored on our own server and emails are sent via our email provider (Resend) — only to deliver the emails you signed up for. One-click unsubscribe in every email.
  • Analytics: cookieless attribution (footprint-v2.js) to our own collector; Meta Pixel / Google tags only with your consent.

When you click through to a merchant (e.g. Amazon), their privacy policy governs what happens next — we are not involved in that transaction.

3. Your rights and choices

  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails: one click, immediate.
  • Access / correction / deletion: email `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`; handled within 30 days. Note: order records needed for tax law are kept as long as legally required.
  • GDPR (EU/UK): basis is *consent* (newsletter, analytics). Withdraw consent anytime.
  • CCPA (California): we do not sell personal information; right to know/delete/non-discrimination at `{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}`. Full sale/share opt-out mechanics: CCPA Do Not Sell/Share.
  • Cookies: manage anytime via "Cookie settings" in the footer — see Cookie Policy.

4. Emails

There are no order emails — we sell nothing. If you join the newsletter, you get the emails you signed up for (preset delivery, photo tips, new picks). Marketing emails only go to subscribers; unsubscribe is one click, immediate.

5. Orders, returns, refunds

We handle no orders and therefore no returns or refunds. If you bought through a merchant link, their return/refund policy applies — contact the merchant directly.

6. Children

Not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes
