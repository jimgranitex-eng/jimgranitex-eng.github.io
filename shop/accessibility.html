Accessibility

Our commitment

We want everyone to use this store. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

What this means in practice

  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and checkout
  • Text alternatives for product images
  • Labeled form fields and sufficient color contrast
  • A cookie banner you can operate by keyboard

Feedback

If you hit a barrier, email the support address in the footer with the page URL and what happened — we fix accessibility issues as bugs, not feature requests.

Third parties

Checkout and payments run on Stripe, which maintains its own accessibility program.
