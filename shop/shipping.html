Shipping

Every product is print-on-demand: it's printed and made when you order, then shipped by the maker (Printful / Printify).

Timing

Production: 2–7 business days, plus transit time. You'll get a tracking number by email when your order ships.

Where we ship

US and EU only. Other zones are turned off at checkout — this is deliberate: the first version of this store learned that long-haul shipping costs kill the experience, so we'd rather not sell than surprise you.

Rates

Shipping is calculated at checkout from the maker's live rates. Free US shipping on orders over $50.

Tracking & problems

Lost or stalled package? Email us (address in the footer) with your order number — we chase it with the maker and reprint or refund if it's gone.
