FAQ

How long does shipping take?

Print-on-demand: 2–7 business days to make, plus transit. Tracking by email when it ships.

Do you ship to my country?

US and EU only, for now. Other zones are off at checkout.

What if my item arrives damaged or misprinted?

Photo + order number to our support email within 30 days → free reprint or full refund, your call.

How do pet sizes work?

Measure neck (bandanas) or chest (hoodies/sweatshirts) and match the size chart on the product page. When in doubt, size up.

Where's my tracking number?

It's emailed when the maker ships. Check spam — then email us and we'll resend it.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders go to print fast. Email us immediately with your order number — if it hasn't printed yet, we'll stop it.

Do you have a phone number?

No — email only. It's how we keep every support interaction written, tracked, and answered.
