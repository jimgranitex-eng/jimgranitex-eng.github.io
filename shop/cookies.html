1. What cookies and tracking we use

What Meta Pixel collects (per Meta's documentation): page views and the standard + custom events listed above, device/browser information, IP address, and Meta cookie IDs (`_fbp`, `_fbc`). The shipped snippet does NOT enable advanced matching (no hashed email/phone fields are sent); if advanced matching is ever enabled, this policy is updated first.

What Google tags collect (per Google's documentation): page-view and event data with the custom op/placement/campaign parameters, device/browser information, approximate location from IP, and first-party cookie IDs (`_ga`, `_ga_<id>`). GA4 does not log individual IP addresses (per Google's documentation).

2. The consent rule

  • EU/UK/EEA: consent-first — no analytics, marketing, or attribution tag fires until banner "Accept." Default denied. See Consent Banner Spec.
  • California: opt-out — "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link honors sale/sharing opt-outs (Meta cross-context advertising counts).
  • Cookie settings link in the footer re-opens preferences anytime.

3. Note on platform cookies (self-built — no third-party platform)

There is no third-party store platform: operational cookies are set by our own storefront code. Our banner controls the tags we add on top (Meta, Google, footprint.js). Stripe Checkout sets its own strictly-necessary payment cookies during checkout.
