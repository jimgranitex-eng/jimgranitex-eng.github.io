Why this one

  • The HERO 25K sensor is genuinely top-tier: no acceleration, no smoothing, no angle snapping — the specs competitive players actually check.
  • 11 programmable buttons with onboard memory: MMO players bind everything; everyone else uses the two thumb buttons and the hyper-fast scroll wheel.

The honest catch

It's on the heavier side even without extra weights — ultralight FPS purists (sub-70g crowd) will find it chunky. And the shape is love-it-or-hate-it for small hands.

Who should buy it

  • Palm/claw grippers with medium-large hands

Who should skip it

Buyer questions, answered

Wired or wireless?
The HERO is wired. The G502 X Lightspeed is the wireless version at a higher price.

Is it good for work too?
Yes — the buttons map to copy/paste, macros, and app shortcuts. It's a cult productivity mouse too.

How's the scroll wheel?
Dual-mode: clicky notched or hyper-fast free-spin. Owners consistently call it the best wheel on any mouse.
