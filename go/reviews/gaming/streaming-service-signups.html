Questions, answered

Which service has a free tier?
Spotify and Peacock both publish free, ad-supported tiers. Apple Music and Crunchyroll's premium content require paid plans (Crunchyroll also keeps a limited free option).

Can I cancel easily?
Yes — all four manage billing in your own account on their official sites, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.

Is there one service that covers music, anime, and TV?
No. These four cover different lanes — that's the point of the roundup. Pick the one or two that match how you actually spend your time.
