Questions, answered

Are cheap game-key sites safe?
Many are not. Gray-market resellers let anonymous sellers list keys of unknown origin — those keys can be bought with stolen payment methods and later revoked by the publisher. Buying from an authorized store is the only way to be sure your key stays valid.

Can I return a game I bought on Steam?
Steam's standard policy covers refunds for recent purchases with limited playtime — the exact window is published on their support pages. Gift cards and keys redeemed outside Steam follow the seller's policy.

Do Humble Bundle keys activate on Steam?
Most Humble Bundle game keys are Steam keys — the bundle page states the platform for every item before you buy.
