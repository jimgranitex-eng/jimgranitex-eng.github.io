Why this one

  • The attachment ecosystem is the moat: pasta rollers, meat grinders, grain mills — dozens of attachments, most fitting decades of models. No competitor matches it.

The honest catch

It's heavy (around 26 pounds) and lives on the counter — if you don't have counter space, you'll resent it. And the honest truth: if you bake less than twice a month, a $40 hand mixer does the job.

Who should buy it

  • Weekly bakers (bread, cookies, pizza dough)
  • Anyone building a long-term kitchen
  • Attachment-curious cooks (pasta, grinding)

Who should skip it

  • Rare bakers — a hand mixer is fine
  • Tiny kitchens with no counter space
  • Strict budgets (the Mini or a hand mixer covers 80%)

Buyer questions, answered

Artisan vs Professional?
The Artisan is the home sweet spot. The Professional series adds power for very stiff doughs — most home bakers never need it.

Do the attachments fit old models?
Mostly yes — the attachment hub design has been stable for decades. That's the ecosystem advantage.

Is it loud?
It's a mixer, not a whisper. Fine for daytime; your household will know when bread day is.
