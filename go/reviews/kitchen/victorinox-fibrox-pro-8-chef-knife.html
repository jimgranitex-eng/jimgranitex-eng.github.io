Buyer questions, answered

8-inch or 10-inch?
8-inch for almost everyone. The 10-inch only pays off if you regularly break down large produce or portion big cuts.

Can it go in the dishwasher?
No — hand wash. Dishwashers dull edges and can crack handles. Thirty seconds with soap and water.

How often should I sharpen it?
Hone it every few uses, sharpen when honing stops bringing the edge back — typically every few months of home use.
