Buyer questions, answered

Shared vs managed vs cloud — what's the difference?
Shared is cheapest (you share a server), managed WordPress adds maintenance, cloud/managed-cloud scales with traffic. Start shared; move up when speed or traffic demands it.

How much should hosting cost?
Entry plans run a few dollars a month; renewals cost more. Budget for the renewal price, not the intro price.

Can I move hosts later?
Yes — WordPress sites migrate cleanly, and most hosts offer free migration. Don't let fear of moving lock you into a bad host.
