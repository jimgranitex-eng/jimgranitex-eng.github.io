The lineup

Midjourney — Best for AI images
When creators talk about AI images that actually look good, they're usually talking about Midjourney. It's behind a huge share of the AI thumbnails and artwork you see in feeds — the output has a distinctive, polished look that's hard to match.
Best for: Creators who need scroll-stopping visuals and are willing to learn prompting.
The catch: There's no meaningful free tier, and the interface takes a session or two to get comfortable with. You're paying for output quality.

Jasper — Best for AI writing
Jasper positioned itself as the AI writer for marketing teams rather than a general chatbot — templates for ads, video scripts, and posts, plus controls that keep output sounding like your brand.
Best for: Creators treating content as a business who want structured writing help.
The catch: It's priced like business software. If you just need the occasional caption, this is more tool than you need.

Canva AI — Best all-in-one for creators
Canva folded AI writing, image generation, and background tools straight into the editor millions of creators already use. Nothing here is the single best at its job — but having all of it in one tab is the point.
Best for: Creators who want one subscription covering design, copy, and images.
The catch: Jack of all trades: the writing won't beat a dedicated writer and the images won't beat Midjourney. You're buying convenience.

Copy.ai — Best free start
Copy.ai's pitch is simple: start free, automate the repetitive copy (captions, product descriptions, outreach), and upgrade only when the workflows pay for themselves.
Best for: Creators who want to test AI writing without paying first.
The catch: The free tier is genuinely useful but limited — heavy users will hit the ceiling.

Buyer questions, answered

Do I need both a writing tool and an image tool?
If you publish regularly, yes — they solve different bottlenecks. Canva AI is the compromise if you want one subscription instead of two.

Will AI content hurt my SEO or reach?
Platforms care about quality and originality, not which tool you used. Edit everything, add your own angle, and don't publish raw output.

Which one should I try first for free?
Copy.ai and Canva AI both have free tiers worth exploring before you spend anything.
