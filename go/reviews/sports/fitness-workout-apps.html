Questions, answered

Do any of these apps guarantee results?
No — and be skeptical of any app that does. These apps provide workout structure, programming, and tracking. Results depend on consistency and factors no app controls.

Which one is free?
Nike Training Club is free. The others publish free trials or free tiers — check the official site for the current offer before paying.

Do I need equipment?
Nike Training Club and Apple Fitness+ include bodyweight options. Fitbod adapts to whatever equipment you tell it you have.
