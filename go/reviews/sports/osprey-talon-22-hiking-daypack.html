Why this one

  • The AirScape ventilated backpanel is the feature owners mention most: it keeps the pack off your back, which matters on any hike over an hour.
  • 22 liters hits the day-hike sweet spot — layers, lunch, 2L of water, first aid — without tempting you to overpack.
  • Osprey's All Mighty Guarantee (lifetime warranty, any damage, any reason) is the best in the business and owners cite it constantly.

The honest catch

It's not ultralight — around 2.5 pounds empty. Ounce-counters will go lighter. And the hipbelt pockets are snug; big phones barely fit.

Who should buy it

  • Day hikers doing 2–8 hour hikes
  • Anyone who runs hot (the ventilation is the point)
  • Buy-it-for-life gear buyers

Who should skip it

  • Ultralight thru-hikers (look at frameless packs)
  • People who need one bag for travel + hiking (get a travel pack instead)

Buyer questions, answered

Is 22L enough for a full day?
Yes for 3-season day hikes: layers, food, water, first aid, headlamp. Winter or technical gear needs more.

Does it fit as a personal item on planes?
Generally yes — it's carry-on friendly and works as a travel daypack too.

Men's vs women's?
The Tempest is the women's equivalent with adjusted torso and hipbelt geometry.
