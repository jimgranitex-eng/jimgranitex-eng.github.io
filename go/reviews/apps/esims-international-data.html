Questions, answered

Does my phone support eSIM?
Most recent flagship phones do; many budget models don't. Check your phone's specifications or the provider's compatibility page before buying.

What does 'unlimited' actually mean?
Unlimited-data eSIMs have fair-use policies — heavy hotspot use may be throttled. Read the plan's published terms if you'll rely on it as your only connection.

Can I keep my home number?
Yes — eSIMs add a data line alongside your existing SIM, so your home number stays active for calls and texts.
