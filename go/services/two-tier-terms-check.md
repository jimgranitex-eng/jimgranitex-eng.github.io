# Two-Tier Sub-Affiliate Terms Check — GO-57 Item #100

**Purpose:** Terms verification for whether known affiliate programs allow two-tier (sub-affiliate) referrals — i.e., earning an override/bonus on commissions of affiliates you recruit. Researched October 2026.

**Standing position (legal-boundary):** Default posture is **single-tier promotion only**. No promotional content promising overrides may be built unless written confirmation exists from the specific program/brand. No income hype anywhere.

## What "two-tier" means here

Affiliate A recruits Affiliate B into the same program. When B earns a commission, A receives an additional percentage (override/bonus). Distinct from:
- *Volume tiers* (higher rates for your own higher sales) — not two-tier.
- *Sub-affiliate networks* (aggregators routing opaque downstream affiliates) — some brands ban these while allowing transparent two-tier referrals; read each brand's terms.

## Verdicts

| Program | Two-tier allowed? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Associates | **NO — not available** | Single-tier only. Commissions come only from qualifying purchases made through your own tagged links. No sub-affiliate or override mechanism exists in the program. |
| Impact | **CONDITIONAL — per brand program** | The Impact platform supports multi-tier/sub-affiliate tracking, but permission is set by each brand's program terms. Some brands permit sub-affiliate promotion with transparency requirements (see Made By Mary); others forbid it. Must read the specific brand's terms. |
| ShareASale | **HISTORICALLY merchant-level; platform shut down** | ShareASale historically offered two-tier as a merchant-level feature (e.g., Geekbuying's documented two-tier incentive). **ShareASale was shut down by Awin on October 6, 2025** and folded into Awin — no new ShareASale signups exist. Verify two-tier under Awin's terms instead. |
| ClickBank | **NO — not available / not documented** | Single-tier hop-link model: affiliates earn the vendor-set commission on their own referrals only. No published two-tier or sub-affiliate override mechanism. |
| PartnerStack | **CONDITIONAL / not documented as standard** | PartnerStack runs per-vendor partner programs (affiliate, referral, reseller channels). No platform-wide sub-affiliate override is documented for affiliates. PartnerStack's own program pays 15% on the first year of businesses *referred to the platform* — a business-referral commission, not a sub-affiliate override. Verify per vendor program. |
| Digistore24 | **CONDITIONAL — per vendor/product** | Vendors configure commission structures per product on Digistore24. Any second-tier arrangement is vendor-specific, not platform-guaranteed. Verify per offer before recruiting. |

## Recommendation

Do not build any "recruit sub-affiliates" promotional content. The only programs in this list with any two-tier path are **conditional** (Impact per-brand, PartnerStack per-vendor, Digistore24 per-product), and even there, terms must be read for the specific program before any recruiting activity. Amazon Associates and ClickBank have no mechanism at all; ShareASale no longer exists. Promotional promises of overrides are a legal-boundary and FTC risk — **stage nothing until written terms confirm**.
